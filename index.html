Your photos stay on this phone.

FrameLog is a private check-in log. No account. No cloud. Nothing leaves the device unless you export it.

  • Same pose, same light. Ghost overlay lines up each shot with the last one.
  • Numbers next to the frame. Weight and measurements sit on the photo, not in a separate app.
  • Then vs now. Side-by-side or a slider — you pick the two dates.

On iPhone: Safari share sheet → Add to Home Screen. Then it opens like an app.
